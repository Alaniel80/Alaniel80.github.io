Which search engine has the highest market share? I have been planning my next vacation for a while now, and for this assignment, I have searched for “best season to go to Bahamas”. I used quotes on purpose to indicate a specific search and to narrow it down. Across platforms, the results generally pointed to the winter-through-spring dry season, although the presentation and usefulness differ. Google search gave me the most direct answer. I got one Facebook page and an AI summary with links to Travel US news, Reddit, and Travel+Leisure.com. That was the exact information I needed on weather, high and low seasons. Super easy to navigate, too – Google’s interface is simple and fast. Bing search engine gave me 31400 results and a mix of guidelines, activities, videos, and even cruise ads. Although some information was useful, I need to narrow the search further. Yahoo Search did not show the number of results and mostly gave me similar videos with Bing search, airfare deals, resorts, and cruise promotions at the top. To get anything helpful, I had to scroll down. Ask.com officially closed on May 1, 2026; therefore, I cannot include it in the comparison. Overall, Google gave me the most useful and straight results, Bing provided good summaries and related information, and Yahoo was the least useful for this specific search. Using Google as my preferred search engine, I checked current worldwide search-engine market-share data.
