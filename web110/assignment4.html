The Travel+Leisure Newsletter offers hotel reviews, destination rankings, and industry news, giving me valuable insight into consumer preferences and travel market patterns. • Research bulk email restrictions and identify appropriate ways to send bulk email messages. According to PowerDMARK, a bulk email sender is any individual or organization that sends 5,000+ messages per day from the same domain. Typically includes automated notifications, promotions, newsletters, and transactional messages sent through marketing platforms or CRMs. Recipients may or may not have interacted with the sender. Bulk email senders must follow strict rules to avoid spam and phishing. For example, for Google, key requirements will be: • Threshold: 5,000/day • SPF, DKIM, DMARC implementation • One-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058 headers) • Enforces ARC for forwarded mail • Uses permanent 550 errors for non-compliance • Full enforcement since 2025 Source: PowerDMARC.
