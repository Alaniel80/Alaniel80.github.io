1. Research plans available from different wired (cable, DSL) and wireless (cellular, satellite) Internet providers. Compare mobility, availability, bandwidth, reliability, delay, concurrent connections, security, and cost. Starlink, Comcast, and Mint Mobile represent three internet technologies – satellite, cable, and cellular. Because they serve different use cases, the choice depends on where you live and why/how you use the internet. Let us compare them on mobility, availability, bandwidth, reliability, delay, concurrent connections, security, and cost. Mobility: Starlink, fixed dish with portable options.; Comcast - wired(no mobility options); Mint Mobile – full mobility with T-Mobile coverage. Availability: Starlink – almost anywhere; Comcast – Cities and suburbs, aprox 41 states; Mint Mobile – nationwide Bandwidth/Delay: Starlink – DS~130 Mbps/US~15 Mbps/Latency ~25-50 ms (satellite); Comcast – DS ~75-2000 Mbps/US~10-200 Mbps/Latancy~15-30 ms(cable); Mint Mobile – DS~5-200 Mbps (cellular)/US – lower uploads, depending on the tower/Latency~40-60 ms. (Sources: Starlink vs Xfinity: Speed, Latency, Price & When Each Wins (2026), https://www.speedtest.net/reports/united-states/mobile/) Reliability: Starlink- weather-dependent; Comcast - very reliable, consistent performance; Mint Mobile – depends on tower congestion. Concurrent connections: Starlink - 10-20 devices; Comcast - 20-50 devices; Mint Mobile - 1-5 devices. (Numbers come from satellite network characteristics, cable network capacity, and cellular hotspot limitations). Security: Starlink – encrypted satellite link; Comcast – standard cable modem; Mint Mobile – standard cellular encryption. Cost: Starlink ~ 50-175$/month+~600$ hardware (https://starlink.com/service-plans); Comcast~45-100$/month (https://www.xfinity.com/learn/internet-service); Mint Mobile~15-30$/month (https://www.mintmobile.com/plans/). In conclusion, Starlink is the best choice for rural, underserved areas with clear skies, where wired infrastructure is limited. Comcast is one of the best choices for home use, gaming, and video calls. Mint Mobile is designed only for mobile phone data and provides nationwide coverage. Personally, I use Mint Mobile for all of our family’s mobile phones and Mint Home Internet for our household connection, which currently offers the lowest monthly price available. 2. Research and compare license and support costs for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Windows, macOS, and Linux have significant differences in both licensing and support costs. Windows 11 Pro costs around 200$ per device, and Windows Server licensing can cost thousands of dollars due to per-core fees and CAL’s(Client Access Licenses); support cost is medium-high due to regular updates and security patches. macOS has no license cost; it is bundled with Apple hardware; support cost is low-medium because Apple’s ecosystem is tightly integrated and generates fewer support issues. Linux is free to use; enterprise distributions charge for support, not the OS itself; support cost is free-to-high depending on the subscription (Source: https://computingforgeeks.com/linux-vs-windows/, https://www.projectwizards.net/en/blog/2026/08/mac-vs-pc-tco-calculator?utm_source=copilot.com). Windows is the most expensive due to licensing and support requirements but remains essential for certain enterprise applications. macOS offers lower support costs but requires premium hardware. Linux provides the lowest overall cost, particularly for server environments, with flexible support options depending on organizational needs.
