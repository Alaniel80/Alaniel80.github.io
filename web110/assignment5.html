1. Complete the TutorialsPoint: Social Networking. Based on the information provided, decide which of the following social media sites interest you.

The social media sites that interest me most are VK, WhatsApp, and YouTube. VK and WhatsApp are especially important because they help me stay connected with my family and friends in Russia. Both platforms support messaging, media sharing, and community interaction, which makes communication simple and personal. VK also offers an extensive music library and a built-in player, so I can keep up with new music releases from back home. I do not use Instagram or Flickr, and I rarely use Facebook, mainly because I do not have an active social circle on those platforms. As a result, they play no meaningful role in my daily communication.

2. Review Smart Insights: The Honeycomb model. Evaluate each of the most popular social media sites (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Reddit, Snapchat, Tumblr, Twitter, YouTube) based on their level of support for each of the honeycomb framework elements (identity, conversations, sharing, presence, relationships, reputation, groups). Which sites support the full honeycomb framework? Which sites are lacking elements?

For my research, I used Copilot’s help and SimilarWeb. Facebook is the only platform that fully supports all seven elements of the Honeycomb Model, showing strong performance in identity, conversations, sharing, presence, relationships, reputation, and groups. Twitter (X), Instagram, and YouTube differ from Facebook by lacking meaningful group structures. LinkedIn stands out with strong identity and reputation features but provides limited presence indicators and weak group engagement. Pinterest, Reddit, and Tumblr differ from more interactive platforms because they lack conversational tools and real-time presence. Snapchat is unique in emphasizing real-time communication but still offers weak group features.
