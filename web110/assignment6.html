1. Intellectual Property

  • Review RIAA: About Piracy. Research legal issues and potential penalties related to illegal peer-to-peer file sharing.

Illegal P2P file sharing violates U.S. copyright law, specifically the Copyright Act and the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA). The core legal issues include copyright infringement, unauthorized distribution, circumvention of digital protections, and contributory or vicarious infringement. Users can face serious consequences, including large civil fines (thousands of dollars), criminal charges (up to 5 years in prison for first offenses), and service account termination. RIAA enforces copyrights through litigation, ISP cooperation, and monitoring technologies.

Source: Recording Industry Association of America (RIAA). “Enforcement.” https://www.riaa.com/resources-learning/enforcement/. Accessed October 3, 2026.
